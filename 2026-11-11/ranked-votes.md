# Sample Ranked-Choice Vote

Made-up kata ideas for trying out `instantRunoff`. Each voter gets two
votes, a 1st and a 2nd choice, written as `Name: 1st choice > 2nd choice`.

## Ideas

- Conway's Game of Life
- Mastermind code breaker
- Elevator controller
- Mars rover
- Tennis scoring
